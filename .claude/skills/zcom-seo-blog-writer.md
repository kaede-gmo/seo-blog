## Key Rules & Requirements

### 1. Business Problem First Rule (HIGHEST PRIORITY)

Articles must focus on solving business problems.

**GOOD Examples:**
- How to Build More Trust Online
- Ways to Strengthen Your Online Presence
- Building Credibility for Small Businesses
- Growing Beyond Social Media

**BAD Examples:**
- What Is SSL?
- What Is Web Hosting?
- Best Hosting Providers

**Target Ratio:**
- 80% Business Problem + Solution
- 20% Product Discussion

### 2. Diagnose Before Recommend Rule

Before recommending themes, plugins, hosting, or products:
1. How to identify the problem
2. How to evaluate the problem
3. How to fix the problem

Only after these steps should recommendations appear. **Teach before recommending.**

### 3. Helpful Content Rule

**Self-test:** If all product mentions were removed, would this article still provide substantial value? If NO, rewrite.

### 4. Positive Content Rule

Content must educate, support, and empower readers. Never use fear-based, shame-based, or emotionally manipulative language.

**Avoid:**
- Your Website Is Costing You Customers
- Why Your Business Is Failing Online
- You're Losing Sales Every Day
- You Are Doing It Wrong

**Prefer:**
- How to Build More Customer Trust Online
- Ways to Improve Your Online Presence
- Growing Beyond Social Media

**When discussing limitations:**
1. Acknowledge the strengths of the current approach
2. Explain where it works well
3. Explain where additional improvements may help
4. Provide practical next steps
5. Focus on opportunities rather than problems

### 5. E-E-A-T Rule

Every article must demonstrate:
- **Experience:** Real-world scenarios
- **Expertise:** Technical knowledge and best practices
- **Authoritativeness:** Industry credibility
- **Trustworthiness:** Verifiable facts, no hallucinations

### 6. Fact Verification Rule

**NO HALLUCINATIONS.** Never invent statistics, research, studies, benchmarks, or customer numbers. If information cannot be verified, rephrase or remove it.

### 7. Philippines Context Rule (CRITICAL)

Articles targeting Philippines must include **at least three Philippines-specific examples:**
- Facebook-first businesses
- Messenger inquiries
- Online sellers
- Home-based businesses
- Local service providers
- Mobile-first users
- Customers discovering businesses through Facebook groups
- Businesses accepting GCash payments
- Businesses competing with larger brands

References must feel natural—not forced. Readers should clearly feel the article was written for Philippine businesses.

### 8. Competitor Research

Analyze competitor content internally (research step only).

**Never output competitor analysis unless explicitly requested.**

Example sources:
- godaddy.com/resources
- namecheap.com/blog
- hostinger.com/tutorials
- bluehost.com/blog
- wpbeginner.com

**Continue immediately to writing the complete article after research.**

## Content Structure & Format

### Blog Metadata Block (MANDATORY - Must appear FIRST)

```
SEO Title: [50-60 characters, include primary keyword]
Meta Description: [140-160 characters, include primary keyword]
Featured Image Concept: [description]
Visual Composition: [composition details]
Suggested Overlay Text: [max 8 words]
Alt Text: [descriptive]
Recommended Size: 1200x630
Estimated Reading Time: [based on 200 words/min]
Target Audience: [from brief]
Primary Keyword: [from brief]
```

### WebPro SEO Fields (MANDATORY - Appears after metadata block)

```
title: [matches SEO Title]
description: [matches Meta Description]
og:title: [optimized for social]
og:description: [optimized for social]
og:type: article
robots: index,follow
llms_category: [short English category]
llms_name: [short English page name]
llms_explanation: [20-40 words summary]
```

### Article Structure

1. **H1** (exactly one)
2. **Executive Summary** (exactly 3 bullets, max 8 words each, max 24 words total)
3. **Introduction** (120-180 words, max 220 words)
4. **Main Content** (H2 sections with H3 subsections as needed)
5. **FAQ Section** (3-5 questions, 50-120 words per answer)
6. **Conclusion** (80-150 words, max 180 words)
7. **CTA Section** (benefit statement + action statement + button)

### Heading Requirements

**H1:**
- Exactly one per article
- Use naturally in article flow

**H2:**
- 4-8 words target
- 10 words maximum
- 5-6 H2 sections per article

**H3:**
- 2-4 H3 sections per article
- Support H2 headings

**NO manually generated heading ids or anchor links—let WordPress TOC plugin handle this.**

### Content Length

- **Target:** 1200-1800 words (ideal 1400-1600)
- **If desired_word_count specified:** ±5% tolerance
- **Reading time:** Round up from (total words ÷ 200)

### Executive Summary

Format:
```html
<div class="zcom-exec-summary">
  <strong>In This Article:</strong>
  <ul>
    <li>✓ Point 1 (max 8 words)</li>
    <li>✓ Point 2 (max 8 words)</li>
    <li>✓ Point 3 (max 8 words)</li>
  </ul>
</div>
```

### Introduction Length

- Target: 120-180 words
- Maximum: 220 words
- Must reach main content quickly

### FAQ Section

- **Count:** 3-5 questions minimum
- **Answer length:** 50-120 words per answer
- **Structure:** Each Q/A as separate h3/p pair
- **Schema:** Compatible with FAQPage schema plugins (RankMath, Yoast SEO)

```html
<h3>Question Here?</h3>
<p>Answer text here, 50-120 words.</p>
```

### Conclusion

- Target: 80-150 words
- Maximum: 180 words
- Summarize main problem, lesson, and next action
- No new topics introduced

### CTA Section (EXACTLY ONE)

Structure:
1. Benefit Statement (1-2 sentences)
2. Action Statement (1-2 sentences)
3. CTA Button (action-oriented)

**CTA Button Examples:**
- Build Your Website
- Get Started Today
- Launch Your Business Online
- View Plans
- Get Professional Email

**Avoid:** Learn More, Click Here, Submit, Continue
